## Decisions
- Filter order: speed → acceleration → hard constraints (workspace, normal displacement, force) by alternating projection, with zero as the fallback (zero is admissible inside the safe set). Workspace and normal displacement use a discrete-time barrier (next-step containment), so there is no extra CBF gain parameter.
- Force limit: at or above `max_contact_force_n`, no velocity component into the tissue; retreat is allowed.
- Normal axis: admittance law v_z = k_f (F_ref − F), with the set-point switched by m̂. LIFT retracts at a fixed speed. The policy cannot command force: its chunk enters only the tangential/orientation channels.
- The UR driver interface was not chosen (docs not readable); command topic/type are human-filled config values.
