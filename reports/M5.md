## Decisions
- One bag = one episode. Master clock = B-mode header stamps; pose interpolated (linear + slerp), wrench linear; per-step actions from the interpolated poses.
- Hold start = an `arrival` event. The teleop node emits it when the motion executor reports arrival on `/teleop/arrived`, and once at node start for station 0.
- Converted `contact_cmd` after a LIFT key stays `LIFT` until the next arrival (keys alone do not mark recontact). `backtrack` is derived from station order; it is unknown (False) for the last hold of a bag.
- Station order in `plan_session` is sequential (0, 1, 2, …); the scheduler asks for LIFT with probability `p_lift` (default 0). The teleop target is always the next station. **Human**: does the protocol include back-tracking or lifts on cue?
- Supported B-mode type: `sensor_msgs/msg/Image` mono8 only, until I can read micrus.
