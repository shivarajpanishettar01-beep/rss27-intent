## Decisions
- **Synthetic station spacing changed from 2 cm to 5 cm** (hold-world default). With 2 cm at 10–30 mm/s, most sweep chunks span the deceleration, so constant velocity overshoots: B2 9.65 mm vs B1 8.15 mm mean sweep error. With 5 cm: B2 2.98 mm vs B1 8.36 mm; with 8 cm: 1.10 vs 7.80. The acceptance test was not changed. This is a real property of CV extrapolation on short sweeps; **it may also show up on the rig**, depending on the real station spacing and speeds. M1 tests re-pass with the new default.
- **Timing of late commits.** In a recording the robot moves on at the key press, so a blind policy that would leave *after* D cannot be timed from the recording. `hold_timing` extends the hold counterfactually by repeating the last in-hold observation with advancing time and `e_proprio`. This is exact for pose/force/e-based models (B1–B4, B6) and approximate for image policies, whose late behaviour is measured in closed loop (M10).
- **B4**: knows med(D) (the p_D median here; on cued data use the training-split median) and `e_proprio`; outside holds it outputs its departure chunk (only hold behaviour is specified, and it is scored on holds only).
- **B3** runs in the tool frame of the pose at filter reset; HOLD/CV/LIFT as specified; noise parameters (measurement 0.1 mm, velocity diffusion 2 mm/s per step) are hyper-parameters, not rig facts. The transition matrix is a constructor argument, estimated from training-split labels (HOLD→HOLD, SWEEP→CV, LIFT/RECONTACT→LIFT).
- **B5**: decorrelation gives |d| only, so the sign is reported as +tool x. ρ0 (zero-motion correlation) added to the calibration curve because electronic noise caps ρ below 1.
- **Hold displacement** in the hold metrics is the tangential |[x, y]|; rotations enter only the error metrics.
