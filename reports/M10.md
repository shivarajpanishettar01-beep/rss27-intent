## Decisions
- In the mock, D = the scheduler's sampled duration (reaction time 0), so the theory check is exact; on the rig, D comes from the key press.
- T = first commanded chunk above `thr` after hold start, at the policy period; quantisation biases T by up to one period (hence the 0.02 s allowance at dt = 0.02 s).
- The mock robot does not render images or force, so vessel-in-view, centring, force-in-band, confidence and intervention counts come from rig trial logs (columns `view_frac`, `force_ok`, `kappa`, `interventions`). The metric functions are tested on synthetic arrays.
- The policy server → controller → robot loop is not exercised end to end here (no ROS). **Lab**: a trial-log recorder for the rig (one parquet row per hold) still has to be agreed with you; I have not written it because it depends on the M9 interface choices.
