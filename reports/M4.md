## Decisions
- **Image embedding for leakage**: parameter-free 4×4 pooled intensity (cloud default) + wrench mean/variance over 1 s + coupling indicator + 4 depth-band intensities. ResNet-18 (ImageNet, torchvision) is available given a local weights file. **Human to choose** (SPEC: "a small pretrained CNN or the policy's frozen vision encoder; choose one, pin it").
- **Permutation null**: obs rows permuted among samples in the same 0.5 s bin of e (my reading of "shuffles observation features within holds of matched e bins").
- **Statistic**: CV log-loss of logistic regression with E minus with E+ctx+obs.
- **Context**: "number of stations" = number of holds in the episode (known to the operator from the scheduler plan).
- **GR00T predictions**: I cannot run or read GR00T here, so the lab exports predictions in our parquet format; the LAB_RUN gives the exact contract and a frame check.
- **Hedging prediction** uses the empirical D of the *training* holds (that is what an imitator learns from).
